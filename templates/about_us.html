Smart Bus Travel

About Project

City Bus Ticketing and Tracking Application is designed to make public transport easier and smarter for passengers. This system allows users to book bus tickets and passes digitally without standing in long queues.

Our goal is to provide a simple and user-friendly platform where passengers can easily manage their travel, check their tickets or passes, and access transport services quickly.

The system aims to support smart transportation by improving convenience, reducing waiting time, and making bus travel more efficient for everyone.
